6S UAV Power Distribution Board

A 6S UAV power distribution board with reverse-polarity and transient protection, dual independently regulated 5A rails, and raw ESC power distribution sized to IPC-2152 standards.

80A Combined trunk current
2× TPS5450 Regulated 5A rails
6S Battery input

Currently at the design stage — schematic and PCB layout complete, not yet fabricated.

Overview

Designed a 6S power distribution board for a UAV, splitting power into three paths: a protected and regulated stage for onboard compute and servos, and a raw, high-current stage feeding the ESCs directly. The protection and regulation architecture was validated against two reverse-engineered commercial reference PDBs before finalizing the design.

Input Protection

Reverse-Polarity Protection

  • ROHM RD3L01BATTL1 P-channel MOSFET (60V, 10A, 65mΩ RDS(on)), high-side switching topology

Gate Protection Network

  • 10kΩ pull-down resistor + SMBJ5352B 15V Zener clamp — added after identifying that a bare pull-down would drive the gate beyond its rated VGS at full 6S charge voltage

Overcurrent Protection

  • SFE2150 fast-acting fuse (15A/250V, 1808 SMD)

Transient Suppression

  • SMBJ26A unidirectional TVS diode (26V standoff, 42.1V clamp), clamping voltage spikes from ESC back-EMF before they reach the regulated rails

Regulated Power Rails

Two independently regulated 5A rails, each built around a TPS5450DDA (36V, 5A, non-synchronous buck, PowerPAD SOIC-8):

  • Rail 1 — Fixed 5V/5A: dedicated to onboard companion compute (RDK X3)
  • Rail 2 — Solder-jumper selectable 5V / 7.4V / 12V: feeds the servo bus (payload release + gimbal), with the output set via a 3-position solder-jumper feedback divider so the exact voltage can be chosen at assembly time without a schematic change

Shared design details across both rails:

  • Inductor and output capacitor values recalculated from the TPS5450's own datasheet design equations for the board's real 6S operating voltage, not copied from the datasheet's 12V worked example
  • Catch diode: PDS540-13 (Diodes Inc., 40V/5A Schottky)
  • Inductor: Panasonic ETQP5M330YFC (32.5µH, 7.6A Isat, AEC-Q200 automotive-qualified, low-buzz metal composite core for vibration resistance)
  • Both rails draw from the protected bus downstream of the reverse-polarity/fuse/TVS stage — never directly off raw battery voltage

ESC / Motor Power Distribution

  • Raw, unregulated battery bus feeding four ESC connectors — deliberately unprotected on this branch, matching verified industry practice (validated by reverse-engineering two commercial reference PDBs before committing to the architecture)
  • Sized for 80A combined trunk current, 20A per motor branch
  • High-current pad footprints and 2oz outer-layer copper, dimensioned using IPC-2152 current-carrying capacity standards
